How a weight is made

Think of a model as a machine covered in knobs. Each knob is one weight: a single number. Follow one real knob from the transformer in Lab 3 through its whole life.

Every knob starts as a small random number, so the model knows nothing. Nobody ever types a value in. We picked a knob that ends up mattering for "River likes Ruby"; all of them are made the same way.

1 · Turn

Training turns the knob, step by step

Training text: "My puppy name is River. River likes Ruby. Ruby likes River." Each step, the model reads all of it, and every knob is turned a little.

When training ends, every knob's value is written into the weights file. From now on the values are frozen.
