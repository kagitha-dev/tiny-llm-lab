The same tiny model as Lab 1, with one change: it reads every word of your input, not only the last one. Each word's row of weights is added up, and the total decides the next token. That is enough to answer “What is my puppy name?”.

Step 3 · Freeze and ask

Ask a question

The saved weights are loaded and frozen. Nothing you do here changes them.

Try a question made from your training text:

1. Read every word of the input

Blue words have a row of saved weights. Crossed-out words were never in training, so they have no row and add nothing.

2. Add up their rows

Each word's row gives a score to every possible next token. The scores are added. Showing the top columns.

3. Softmax turns the totals into chances

Higher total, higher chance. All chances add up to 100%.

1.0 Low temperature sharpens the chances, high flattens them. Top-k keeps only the k most likely tokens.

A simplified model for understanding. Like Lab 1 it has one grid of weights and whole-word tokens, but it reads the whole input by adding the rows of every word in it. Real LLMs also read the whole input, but attention gives each word a different weight and keeps the word order: this model treats “my puppy name is” and “is name puppy my” the same. It can only use words it saw in training, and it answers by continuing your text, so it works best when the question reuses the words of a training sentence. Back to Lab 1.
