Why doesn't it always pick the top token?
Only counts the draws. Nothing is added to the text.

Temperature changes the jar: low makes the top token fill almost every marble, high makes the colours more even. Top-k decides how many different colours are allowed in the jar at all; top-k 1 means only the top token, so it is picked every time.

A simplified model for understanding. Its tokens are whole words, it looks only at the last token, and it uses one grid of weights. Large language models use subword tokens, billions of weights and the whole input to compute their scores, but follow the same cycle: random weights, training that adjusts them, a saved weights file, and frozen weights that score every possible next token.
